# Case Study: 58-Year-Old Female with Abnormal Screening Mammogram

## Patient Information

**Demographics:**
- 58-year-old female
- High school teacher
- Divorced, two adult children

**Chief Complaint:**
- Routine screening mammogram with Digital Breast Tomosynthesis (DBT)
- No breast symptoms or complaints

## History of Present Illness

Patient presented for routine annual screening mammography. She denies any breast pain, nipple discharge, skin changes, or palpable masses. Her last screening mammogram was 14 months ago and was reported as negative (BI-RADS 1). She has no personal history of breast procedures other than bilateral breast cyst aspirations 12 years ago. She has been meticulous about annual screenings since age 40.

## Past Medical History

- Hypertension (controlled)
- Osteopenia diagnosed 2 years ago
- Migraine headaches
- Prior cesarean sections (2)

## Medications

- Lisinopril 10mg daily
- Alendronate 70mg weekly
- Calcium 1200mg daily with vitamin D3
- Sumatriptan as needed for migraines
- Menopausal hormone therapy (estrogen/progesterone) for 3 years, discontinued 4 years ago

## Allergies

- Sulfa drugs (rash)

## Family History

- Mother diagnosed with breast cancer at age 62 (unknown type)
- Maternal aunt with breast cancer at age 70
- Paternal grandmother with "female cancer" - details unknown
- Sister (age 61) with no history of cancer
- No known BRCA mutations or genetic testing in family

## Social History

- Never smoker
- Alcohol: 3-4 glasses of wine per week
- Menarche at age 12
- G2P2, first full-term pregnancy at age 28
- Natural menopause at age 51
- Hormone replacement therapy for 3 years post-menopause (ages 52-55)
- Regular physical activity (yoga, swimming)
- BMI: 27

- **Breast:** No pain, masses, nipple discharge, or dimpling

**Vital Signs:**
- Blood Pressure: 128/82 mmHg

## Diagnostic Studies

**Current Screening Digital Mammography with DBT:**
- **Right Breast:** Heterogeneously dense breast tissue (ACR category C: 51-75% glandular). No suspicious findings.
- **Left Breast:** Heterogeneously dense breast tissue. 9mm spiculated mass in the upper outer quadrant at the 2 o'clock position, 6cm from the nipple. The mass has irregular margins and is associated with architectural distortion. Several pleomorphic microcalcifications (approximately 15 in number) are noted within and adjacent to the mass in a segmental distribution spanning approximately 12mm. The microcalcifications are heterogeneous in morphology with fine linear and branching forms.
- **Tomosynthesis (DBT) Contribution:** The spiculated mass is significantly more conspicuous on DBT compared to 2D digital mammography, with improved visualization of the radiating spiculations[4][7]. The extent of the associated microcalcifications is better defined on the thin-slice DBT images[5][8].
- **Impression:** BI-RADS Category 5 (Highly suggestive of malignancy)

**Targeted Ultrasound (Left Breast):**
- Corresponding to mammographic finding, there is an irregular hypoechoic mass with angular margins, heterogeneous internal echoes, and posterior acoustic shadowing at the 2 o'clock position, 6cm from the nipple.
- Mass measures 10mm × 8mm × 7mm.
- No abnormal vascularity on color Doppler imaging.
- No abnormal axillary lymph nodes identified.

## Additional Workup

**Ultrasound-guided Core Needle Biopsy of Left Breast Mass:**
- **Procedure:** Under sterile conditions and local anesthesia, ultrasound-guided 14-gauge core needle biopsy performed. Six core samples obtained. Post-procedure mammogram shows metallic clip deployed at biopsy site.
- **Pathology Results:**
  - Invasive ductal carcinoma, grade 2, with associated ductal carcinoma in situ (DCIS), intermediate grade, cribriform and micropapillary patterns with comedo necrosis.
  - Immunohistochemistry:
    - Estrogen Receptor (ER): Positive (>95% strong staining)
    - Progesterone Receptor (PR): Positive (>90% moderate staining)
    - HER2/neu: Negative (1+ by IHC)
    - Ki-67: 12% (low proliferation index)
  - Molecular Subtype: Luminal A-like

**Breast MRI:**
- Left breast mass at 2 o'clock position demonstrating irregular shape, spiculated margins, and heterogeneous enhancement with Type III kinetics (rapid initial rise with washout).
- No additional suspicious enhancing lesions in either breast.
- No evidence of chest wall invasion.
- No abnormal axillary lymph nodes.
- BI-RADS Category 6 (Known malignancy)

**Staging Workup:**
- Chest X-ray: No evidence of metastatic disease
- Complete blood count: Within normal limits
- Comprehensive metabolic panel: Within normal limits

## Assessment and Plan

**Assessment:**
58-year-old female with newly diagnosed left breast cancer detected on screening mammography with DBT. The imaging findings of a spiculated mass with microcalcifications are highly characteristic of malignancy[4][6][7], and in this case correlate with the pathologic diagnosis of invasive ductal carcinoma with DCIS. The spiculated appearance on imaging is associated with the luminal A molecular subtype[9][10], consistent with the immunohistochemistry findings in this case. The patient's tumor is classified as:
- T1b (tumor >5mm but ≤10mm in greatest dimension)
- N0 (no clinical evidence of regional lymph node metastases)
- M0 (no clinical evidence of distant metastases)
- Stage IA breast cancer, luminal A-like subtype (ER+/PR+/HER2-/low Ki-67)

The detection of this small invasive cancer with associated DCIS demonstrates the advantage of DBT over standard digital mammography, particularly for spiculated masses that may be obscured by overlapping tissue on conventional 2D imaging[4][7].

**Plan:**

1. **Surgical Management:**
   - Discuss breast-conserving surgery (lumpectomy) with sentinel lymph node biopsy
   - Review option of mastectomy per patient preference
   - Preoperative consultation with radiation oncology to discuss adjuvant radiation therapy if breast conservation is chosen

2. **Multidisciplinary Tumor Board Presentation:**
   - Present case at multidisciplinary breast conference for treatment planning recommendations

3. **Adjuvant Therapy Planning:**
   - Medical oncology consultation to discuss adjuvant endocrine therapy given ER/PR positive status
   - Oncotype DX testing of surgical specimen to guide decisions about chemotherapy benefit

4. **Genetic Counseling:**
   - Refer for genetic counseling given family history of breast cancer (mother and maternal aunt)
   - Consider germline testing for hereditary breast cancer syndromes

5. **Supportive Care:**
   - Social work consultation for psychosocial support
   - Provide educational materials about breast cancer diagnosis and treatment options
   - Discuss fertility preservation options if applicable

6. **Follow-up:**
   - Surgical consultation scheduled for 3 days
   - Discuss surgical options and timing
   - Post-surgical follow-up planning based on pathology results and staging

**Patient Education:**
- Provided information about invasive ductal carcinoma with DCIS
- Discussed favorable prognostic features (small size, node-negative, ER/PR+, HER2-)
- Reviewed treatment timeline and expected outcomes
- Provided written materials about breast cancer and treatment options
- Explained importance of DBT in detecting this subtle finding that might have been missed on conventional mammography alone[4][5][7]
