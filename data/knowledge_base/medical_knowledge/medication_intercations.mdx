# Radiology-Specific Medication Interactions Reference

## Contrast Media Interactions

### Iodinated Contrast Agents

| Medication Class | Interaction Mechanism | Clinical Implications | Management Protocol |
|-------------------|-----------------------|-----------------------|---------------------|
| **Metformin** | Contrast-induced nephropathy risk in renal impairment [2][6] | Lactic acidosis risk if renal function declines post-procedure [5][6] | - Continue metformin if eGFR ≥45 mL/min [2][6]<br>- Withhold 48h if eGFR <45 or intra-arterial contrast [6] |
| **NSAIDs** | Prostaglandin inhibition reduces renal perfusion [4][8] | Increased contrast-induced nephropathy (CIN) risk [4][8] | Discontinue 24h pre-procedure in patients with eGFR <60 mL/min [4] |
| **Diuretics** | Volume depletion exacerbates contrast nephrotoxicity [4][8] | Acute kidney injury risk in CT angiography [8] | Hydrate 12h pre/post-procedure; consider holding morning dose [8] |

### Gadolinium-Based Contrast Agents (GBCA)

| Medication Class | Interaction Mechanism | Clinical Implications | Management Protocol |
|-------------------|-----------------------|-----------------------|---------------------|
| **Nephrotoxic Drugs** | Cumulative renal injury risk [4][7] | Increased NSF risk in renal impairment [2][4] | Use group II GBCA if eGFR <30 mL/min [2] |
| **Iron Supplements** | Competitive chelation [7] | Altered contrast biodistribution | Hold oral iron 48h prior to liver MRI [7] |
