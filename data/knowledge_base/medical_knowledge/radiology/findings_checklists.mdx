# Radiology Normal Findings Checklists

## Chest X-ray (CXR) Systematic Interpretation Checklist

### Preliminaries
- **Patient Demographics**: Confirm patient name, age, date, time[6]
- **Technical Factors**: PA vs AP, erect vs supine, inspiration quality[6][15]
- **Image Quality Assessment (RIPE)**[6]:
  - **R**otation: Medial clavicle ends equidistant from spinous process
  - **I**nspiration: 5-6 anterior ribs visible at midclavicular line, or 8-10 posterior ribs above diaphragm
  - **P**osition: Scapulae outside lung fields
  - **E**xposure: Thoracic vertebrae visible through heart shadow, IV disc spaces visible

### Systematic Review
1. **Airway & Mediastinum**[6][21]
   - Trachea: Central or slightly right-deviated position
   - Carina and main bronchi: Normal bifurcation at T4-T6 level
   - Mediastinal width: <8cm on PA film
   - Aortic knuckle: Well-defined contour, normal position
   - Hilar structures: Left hilum typically higher (2cm) and more square than right

2. **Breathing: Lung Fields**[4][6][21]
   - Lung markings present throughout all zones
   - Symmetrical appearance between lungs
   - Normal tapering of vessels toward periphery
   - No focal opacities or lucencies
   - Horizontal fissure visible on right lung
   - No signs of pneumothorax (lung markings extend to pleural edge)

3. **Circulation: Heart**[6][15]
   - Position: ⅔ to left, ⅓ to right of midline
   - Size: Cardiothoracic ratio <0.5 on PA film
   - Borders: Right border formed by right atrium, left border by left ventricle/atrium
   - Heart contours: Sharp and well-defined

4. **Diaphragm**[6][15]
   - Right hemidiaphragm higher than left (~2.5cm/1 intercostal space)
   - Smooth, well-defined contours
   - Sharp costophrenic and cardiophrenic angles
   - Gastric bubble visible under left hemidiaphragm

5. **Soft Tissues, Bones & Extras**[6][15]
   - Ribs, sternum, spine, clavicles: No fractures or lytic lesions
   - Breast shadows: Symmetrical
   - Artifacts: Note any ECG leads, tubes, lines, pacemakers
   - Subcutaneous tissues: No emphysema or abnormal swelling

6. **Hidden Areas** (Particularly Important)[21]
   - Area behind heart
   - Lung apices
   - Below diaphragm
   - Lateral costophrenic angles

## Non-contrast Head CT Systematic Interpretation Checklist

### Technique and Quality
- **Technical Parameters**: Sequential axial imaging, 5mm slice thickness[7]
- **Image Quality**: Freedom from motion artifact, complete coverage

### Systematic Review (Outside-In Approach)
1. **Scalp and Skull**[9][22]
   - Scalp: Normal thickness, no hematoma
   - Calvarium: Intact, normal thickness
   - Skull base: No fractures
   - Sinuses: Clear or normal mucosal thickening

2. **Extra-axial Spaces**[7][9][22]
   - Subdural space: No hyperdense collections
   - Subarachnoid space: Cisterns patent, no hyperdensity
   - Epidural space: No collections

3. **Ventricles**[8][9]
   - Lateral ventricles: Normal size, symmetrical
   - Third ventricle: Midline, normal size
   - Fourth ventricle: Midline, normal size
   - No hydrocephalus

4. **Brain Parenchyma**[8][9][22]
   - Grey-white matter differentiation: Preserved
   - Hemispheres: Symmetrical
   - No hypodense or hyperdense foci
   - No mass effect or midline shift
   - No sulcal effacement

5. **Brainstem and Cerebellum**[8][9]
   - Midbrain, pons, medulla: Normal appearance
   - Cerebellum: Normal attenuation, no masses
   - Cerebellopontine angles: Clear cisterns

6. **Vascular Structures**[22]
   - Circle of Willis: No hyperdense vessels
   - No vessel abnormalities
   - No vascular malformations

7. **Key Anatomical Landmarks**[17]
   - Basal ganglia: Normal density
   - Thalami: Symmetrical
   - Internal capsule: Normal appearance
   - Corpus callosum: Intact

## Abdominal Ultrasound Systematic Interpretation Checklist

### Technique and Patient Preparation
- **Patient Preparation**: Fasting status, bladder filling as needed[10]
- **Probe Selection**: Curved array for deep structures, linear for superficial[11]
- **Patient Position**: Supine, lateral decubitus as needed

### Systematic Review (Standard Sequence)[18]
1. **Liver**[10][11][12]
   - Size: Normal range (5cm SAG, 16cm TRV, 15cm AP)[12]
   - Contour: Smooth
   - Parenchyma: Homogeneous, isoechoic to renal cortex
   - Vasculature: Portal vein (<13mm), hepatic veins (<10mm)
   - No focal lesions

2. **Biliary System**[10][12][18]
   - Gallbladder: Normal size (8-9cm SAG, 5cm TRV, 5cm AP)
   - Wall thickness: <3mm
   - No stones or sludge
   - CBD: ≤6mm diameter (add 1mm per decade after age 60)
   - Intrahepatic ducts: Not dilated

3. **Pancreas**[11][12]
   - Head: 2.5cm AP diameter
   - Body: 2.0cm AP diameter
   - Tail: 2.0cm AP diameter
   - Homogeneous echogenicity (slightly hyperechoic to liver)
   - Pancreatic duct: ≤3mm in head, ≤2mm in body

4. **Spleen**[11][12]
   - Size: <12cm SAG, <7cm TRV, <5cm AP
   - Homogeneous parenchyma
   - No focal lesions

5. **Kidneys**[10][12]
   - Size: 9-13cm SAG, 4-5cm TRV, 2.5-4.0cm AP
   - Cortical thickness: ≥1cm
   - No hydronephrosis
   - No stones or masses

6. **Bladder**[10]
   - Wall thickness: <3mm when distended
   - No masses or stones
   - Visualize distal ureters if indicated

7. **Aorta and IVC**[11][12]
   - Aorta: <2.5cm diameter
   - IVC: Caliber varies with respiration
   - Normal flow on color Doppler

8. **Additional Structures**[10][11]
   - Adrenals: Normal size if visualized
   - Bowel: Wall thickness <4mm, no free fluid
   - No abnormal lymphadenopathy

## Axial Body CT Systematic Interpretation Checklist

### Technique and Quality
- **Technical Parameters**: Slice thickness, contrast phase if applicable
- **Coverage**: Complete imaging of required anatomy

### Systematic Review (Methodical Approach)[19][23]
1. **Lower Chest** (if included)[19]
   - Lung bases: Clear
   - No pleural or pericardial effusion
   - Pulmonary vessels: Normal caliber

2. **Liver and Biliary System**[13][19]
   - Liver: Normal size, morphology, and enhancement
   - No masses or contour abnormalities
   - Vasculature: Patent portal and hepatic veins
   - Gallbladder: Normal wall thickness
   - Bile ducts: Normal caliber

3. **Pancreas**[19]
   - Normal morphology and enhancement
   - No ductal dilation
   - No masses or peripancreatic fluid

4. **Spleen**[19]
   - Normal size and enhancement
   - No focal lesions

5. **Adrenals**[19]
   - Normal morphology
   - No masses

6. **Kidneys, Ureters, and Bladder**[19]
   - Kidneys: Normal size, enhancement
   - No hydronephrosis
   - No stones or masses
   - Bladder: Normal wall thickness

7. **GI Tract and Peritoneum**[19]
   - Stomach and duodenum: Normal appearance
   - Small and large bowel: Normal caliber and wall thickness
   - No free fluid or air
   - Normal appendix

8. **Vasculature**[19]
   - Aorta: Normal caliber
   - Patent visceral arteries
   - Patent IVC and portal venous system

9. **Lymph Nodes**[19]
   - No pathologic adenopathy in:
     - Upper abdomen
     - Retroperitoneum
     - Mesentery
     - Pelvis

10. **Reproductive Organs**[19]
    - Males: Normal prostate and seminal vesicles
    - Females: Normal uterus and adnexa

11. **Musculoskeletal**[19]
    - No aggressive osseous lesions
    - No fractures
    - Normal vertebral body heights
